Honest answers

Is it written by AI?

Articles are drafted with AI, and a machine checks every quote against the transcript. Our own software picks and cuts the clips.

Do you ever invent quotes?

Never. A claim we cannot trace to the episode is left out or marked for you to check.

Will it get us views or leads?

We promise no views, followers, traffic or leads, and we never publish anything for you.

Can we talk on a call?

Support is in writing, by email. We do not do calls.

Who sees a sample before we buy?

Only you. A sample is on an unlisted page: it is not linked from our site and search engines are asked not to index it, but it has no password, so anyone with the link can open it. We never post it anywhere else.

Who is it not for?

Teams that already write up and clip every episode in house, and anyone who wants calls or a place in their Slack.

Privacy

We keep the emails and files you send us only to do the work, and delete them on request.
