The problem

Your team records good episodes, but the ideas stay inside the audio. A writer needs 3 to 6 hours to write up one episode, so it does not happen every week.

What you get each month

  • 4 episodes, 4 articles. 1,200 to 1,500 words each, with a headline and subheads.
  • Quotes checked. Every quote is matched against the transcript and carries its timestamp.
  • Extras per episode. Chapter timestamps, 3 LinkedIn posts and 1 newsletter blurb.
  • Optional clips. 8 to 12 vertical short clips a month (9:16, captions burned in), cut from your video. Needs a video recording.

How it works

  1. Email us 4 episode links (and video files if you want clips).

  3. Ask for one free redo per article or one free recut per clip within 5 business days. A wrong quote or fact is fixed free.

Honest notes

  • Articles are drafted with AI, and a machine checks every quote against the transcript. Our own software picks and cuts the clips.
  • We never invent quotes or facts. A claim we cannot trace to the episode is left out or marked for you to check.
  • We never publish anything for you. We promise no views, followers, traffic or leads.
  • Support is in writing, by email. We do not do calls.
  • A sample we make before you buy is sent only to you, on an unlisted page: it is not linked from our site and search engines are asked not to index it, but it has no password, so anyone with the link can open it. We never post it anywhere else.
